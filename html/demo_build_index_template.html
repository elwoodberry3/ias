// Why I built this

A working version of what your adjusters would use.

Claims processing is paperwork-heavy, deadline-driven, and exactly where an agentic system either proves itself or falls apart. I built this to demonstrate one specific thing most AI demos skip: a system that knows when to stop and ask a human.

The agent extracts the claim, checks coverage against real policy clauses with citations attached, and screens for fraud signals — all visible, step by step, not hidden behind a black box. When the confidence score drops below threshold, the workflow pauses and hands the decision to a person instead of guessing.

Everything below is the real build: the same n8n workflow and dashboard you can click through yourself at the bottom of this page.

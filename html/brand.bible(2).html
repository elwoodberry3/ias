04 Layout Principles

DO

Lead with a white or #F9FAFB canvas. Use the primary color (#0A2E36) as a header band, sidebar, or footer block — never as the full page background on light-mode layouts.

DO

Use the accent emerald (#00E5A3) for exactly one element per visible section — the primary CTA button, an active state, or a critical data callout. It loses its power the moment it appears twice in the same viewport.

DO

Use a 4px left border in #00E5A3 or #3F7266 on callout cards, pull quotes, or stat blocks to inject brand color without painting a whole surface.

DON'T

Don't place the accent emerald on white text or use it as a background for body copy. It fails contrast. Emerald backgrounds carry dark (#0A2E36) text only.

DON'T

Don't use the secondary seafoam (#3F7266) as a CTA button. It reads as decorative, not interactive. Reserve it for secondary icons, dividers, and feature illustration.

WHEN

Dark-mode or full-bleed layouts (video overlays, t-shirt graphics, event banners) may invert — primary (#0A2E36) fills the canvas, white and accent carry the type.

06 Voice & Tone

The brand name is a statement of intent, not an edge case. The voice matches: direct, technically credible, mildly irreverent, never performatively edgy. Speak to the operator who feels the pain — not the executive who signs the check.

Write this

"Your team is spending 3 hours a day on tasks I can eliminate in an afternoon."

Not this

"Leveraging cutting-edge AI-powered automation solutions to synergize your workflow ecosystem."

Write this

"Here's exactly what I built, what it replaced, and what it saves per month."

Not this

"I'm passionate about helping businesses transform their digital journey through innovative automation."

Write this

"// n8n node count: 7. Time saved: 40 hrs/week. Client cost: $0 ongoing."

Not this

"As a thought leader in the automation space, I bring a unique perspective to each engagement."

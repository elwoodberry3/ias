# Facebook Marketplace?  

**What about Facebook marketplace?**  

---   

Facebook Marketplace is the absolute highest-risk platform for an independent buyer, especially for high-ticket items like an iPhone. While it is a fantastic place to buy a used coffee table, it is a absolute minefield for premium electronics. [1, 2, 3] 
Because it operates like a digital garage sale, standard e-commerce card protections often do not apply. If she wants to browse Facebook Marketplace, she needs to know the two major phone-specific traps dominating the platform. [4] 
------------------------------
## 🚨 The Top 2 Facebook Marketplace iPhone Traps## 1. The "Delayed Blacklist" (Stolen Phone) Scam
This is the most common and devastating phone scam on the platform. [1, 5] 

* 
* How it works: A seller meets your daughter in a public place. She inspects the iPhone, pops her SIM card in, and it works perfectly. She hands over the cash or sends a mobile payment. [6] 
* The Trap: The phone was actually stolen, or the seller originally bought it on a payment plan and stopped paying for it. A few days or weeks later, the original owner or carrier reports it stolen. The carrier immediately blacklists the device's IMEI number (bricking the phone). The phone instantly loses all cell signal permanently, transforming into an expensive paperweight. [1, 5, 6] 
* Defense: She must check the phone's unique IMEI number before handing over money. Have her dial *#06# on the phone's keypad to display the number, then plug it into a free verification site like [StolenPhoneChecker.org](https://stolenphonechecker.org/) to check its carrier status. [5] 
* 

## 2. Highly Convincing Android "Clones"

* 
* How it works: Counterfeit factories produce physical replicas of new iPhones that use real glass and aluminum housings.
* The Trap: The internal software is actually a heavily modified, "skinned" version of Android designed to look identical to Apple iOS.
* Defense: Have her tap the App Store icon. If it redirects her to a web browser page or a Google sign-in instead of the native Apple App Store ecosystem, it is an Android fake. [7, 8] 
* 

------------------------------
## 🛠️ The Ultimate Rule of Marketplace: No Shipping & No Mobile Payment Apps
If she decides to buy a device on Facebook Marketplace, she must follow these non-negotiable safety rules:

* 
* In-Person & Cash Only: She should never buy a shipped item on Facebook Marketplace, and she should never send a deposit via Venmo, Cash App, or Zelle to "hold" an item. Scammers will make up elaborate stories (e.g., "My dad is moving to a nursing home and I need a deposit before we meet") to get her to send money un-protected. [9, 10] 
* Meet at a "Safe Exchange Zone": She should never meet a stranger at a private home or dark parking lot. Most local police stations have designated, heavily monitored "Safe Exchange Zones" in their public parking lots explicitly for Facebook Marketplace transactions. If a seller refuses to meet at a police station parking lot, cancel the deal immediately. [2, 7, 11]

### OVERVIEW {#overview}

The Prompt Divide explores the hyper-accelerated evolution of software development in the age of agentic AI. The film places Claude Code at the absolute center of a modern, enterprise-grade tech stack that includes n8n workflow automation, Next.js web frameworks, Vercel cloud deployment, and Tailwind CSS. It investigates the high-stakes reality for African American men, who face massive job displacement from AI but possess a historic opportunity to leverage these exact agentic tools for economic sovereignty.

### THE SYNOPSIS {#the-synopsis}

The documentary opens inside modern corporate environments where traditional engineering cycles are shrinking from months to minutes. Through interviews with leading AI researchers, economists, and tech founders, we introduce Claude Code: an agentic AI assistant operating directly inside the terminal to write, test, debug, and deploy code autonomously.

The core conflict highlights a stark economic reality. African American males are disproportionately impacted by the automation of entry-level tech roles, administration, and supply chain logistics. However, the film pivots to an empowering solution. Claude Code effectively democratizes software engineering. By acting as an AI collaborator, it enables individuals to build complex enterprise logic via n8n and launch fast, responsive web apps using Next.js, Vercel, and Tailwind CSS without years of syntax training.

We follow three African American men—an underemployed gig worker, a community organizer, and a traditional IT technician—as they go through an intensive code accelerator. The narrative tracks their journey from prompt novices to elite 'AI orchestrators' who build custom enterprise solutions. Their success proves that terminal literacy and agentic tool mastery are the ultimate keys to economic self-determination.

#### THE MARKET & AUDIENCE {#the-market-&-audience}

* Primary Audience: Black men aged 18–45 looking for rapid, high-utility tech pathways and wealth-building strategies.  
* Secondary Audience: Public broadcasting viewers, educators, impact investors, and workforce development leaders managing AI labor displacement.

#### WHY NOW? {#why-now?}

The traditional software engineer role is changing rapidly. Claude Code represents a paradigm shift where execution speed and system architecture matter more than writing manual code. This documentary serves as an urgent public interest broadcast, showing an underserved community exactly how to control the machines doing the automating.

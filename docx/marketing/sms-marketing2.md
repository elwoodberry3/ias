# SMS Marketing  

PROMPT #1
**Assume I am someone very similar to Nate Herk. What platforms does Nate Herk use to communicate, remind and market to his community**  

---  

[Nate Herk](https://www.google.com/search?q=nate+herk&kgmid=/g/11vf3w7j3g)’s core engine for communication, reminders, and marketing relies heavily on a lean, highly automated "AI Operating System" tech stack. Instead of juggling dozens of disconnected enterprise tools, his ecosystem uses n8n as the central nervous system to sync a few targeted platforms that manage his 450,000+ member audience. [1, 2] 
If you are scaling a community using a similar operational playbook, Nate's exact core channels and infrastructure include the following platforms:
------------------------------
The center of Nate's ecosystem is hosted entirely on [Skool](https://www.skool.com/@nateherk). This is where his free group, AI Automation Society, and his premium tier, AIS+, live natively. [3, 4] 

* Discussion & Classroom: He consolidates all community conversations, leaderboards, resource sharing, and educational modules directly onto Skool. [4, 5] 
* Built-in Reminders: Rather than relying on external calendar apps, he utilizes Skool’s native Community Calendar to schedule live events and Q&A sessions. Skool automatically handles localized time zone notifications and event reminders to optimize attendance. [5, 6] 

------------------------------
Nate does not use traditional middleman integration tools for complex sequences. He is an official [n8n Expert Partner](https://nateherk.com/) and runs his business operations through n8n workflow automations. [2, 7] 

* The Glue: Every webhook from Skool, video upload, or form completion goes through an n8n workflow.
* Data Routing: If a member reaches a milestone or triggers an event, n8n captures that data, runs it through an AI model layer, and logs it or pushes a notification out. [2, 8, 9] 

------------------------------
For rapid, transactional touchpoints, automated operational reminders, and immediate chat interactions, his architectures leverage webhook-friendly messaging interfaces. [8, 10] 

* Telegram Bots: Nate frequently builds and highlights multi-agent architectures that connect straight to Telegram via n8n. This allows users or community admins to prompt AI agents, receive system notifications, and send voice/text reminders through a clean chat interface. [11, 12] 
* Discord: Used for segmented, real-time community chat pipelines and developer updates tied directly to automated repo pushes or member triggers. [8, 10] 

------------------------------
Nate treats content platforms as the top-of-funnel engine that feeds his automated email lists and Skool ecosystem. [1, 13] 

* YouTube & LinkedIn: His primary hubs for long-form tutorials and strategic business positioning.
* Automated Content Repurposing: He writes and designs systems inside n8n that use AI agents to reverse-engineer transcriptions, auto-generate supporting graphics, and draft cross-platform promotional posts for LinkedIn and X (Twitter). [8, 11, 13, 14] 

------------------------------
To keep his marketing, reminders, and user data perfectly aligned without massive overhead, the backend code and workflow data are actively managed by a modern developer stack. [4] 

* Claude Code: He operates out of text/terminal-based AI systems by default to build scripts, format templates, and organize deep context files. [1] 
* Hostinger VPS: Nate utilizes self-hosted virtual private servers to run the underlying background cron jobs, applications, and persistent AI systems that keep his tracking infrastructure running 24/7. [10, 15]

# The Golden Rule of Credit Card Security  

PROMPT  
**I teach enterprise level agentic development with Claude code. I want to write a script for a social media reel/ad that sounds like I’m going to teach you to “scam” and to be a “scammer”…. In the body of the script I tell them I’m joking in a snarky way. Then I end by telling them you do t have to learn to scam when you build a whole idea for real with Claude Code.  The energy you’re using to cut corners could be applied to agentic development… because we will get there faster…like we’re cutting a corner. The tone is a combination of cocky, arrogant and Gary Vee mixed with tough love. Targeting primarily men.**  

---  


The hook can sound **wildly incriminating for the first few seconds**, then flip it: the real “scam” is wasting all that creativity trying to fake a business instead of building one. I’d keep the delivery controlled rather than yelling—the arrogance comes from sounding like you already know where this is going.

**[COLD OPEN — dead serious]**

You wanna learn how to scam?

Good.

I’m gonna teach you how to be a **world-class scammer.**

Not that little bullshit y’all be doing either.

I’m talking enterprise-level shit.

Automation.
AI agents.
Workflows.
Databases.
APIs.

We’re gonna build the whole operation.

**[BEAT — look at camera]**

Relax.

I’m fucking with you.

I’m not teaching you how to scam people.

I’m trying to show you something.

Because some of y’all will spend **six hours figuring out how to make $40 the wrong way...**

but won't spend those same six hours learning how to build something that can make money **for real.**

And that's the part that kills me.

Because you already got the mindset.

You're resourceful.

You know how to find loopholes.

You know how to improvise when shit doesn't work.

You know how to move fast.

You know how to make something outta nothing.

My guy...

**THAT'S DEVELOPMENT.**

You just pointing that shit in the wrong direction.

**[ENERGY BUILDS]**

Give me that same energy in Claude Code.

Instead of figuring out how to fake the product—

**BUILD THE PRODUCT.**

Instead of pretending you got a company—

**BUILD THE COMPANY.**

Instead of faking the website, the backend, the customer experience and the automation—

let's actually build the whole damn thing.

And here's the crazy part:

With agentic development, **we're gonna get there faster anyway.**

So technically...

**we're still cutting corners.**

We're just cutting the **right fucking corners.**

**[CLOSE — slower, direct]**

You don't have to learn how to scam...

when you can take the whole idea in your head—

and build that shit **for real.**

Welcome to Claude Code.

**Now let's build.**

The strongest visual gag would be treating the first 5–7 seconds like a shady late-night “scammer academy” ad, then abruptly breaking character at **“Relax. I’m fucking with you.”** From there, the reel visually transforms into terminals, agents, builds and deployed products.

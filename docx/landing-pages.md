# Landing Pages

PROMPT #1
**I have a VSL. I want to create two more types of landing pages. In 2026, what are the next best funnel types to build?**  

---   

In 2026, pairing a Video Sales Letter (VSL) with alternative top-of-funnel capture architectures is essential for catching the traffic that doesn't have the immediate time or intent to sit through a video. To capture the widest net of high-intent traffic, the two next best funnel types to build alongside your VSL are an Interactive Quiz/Assessment Funnel and a Value-First Lead Magnet Funnel. [1, 2] 
Integrating these specific frameworks captures varying user intents:
## 1. The Interactive Quiz / Assessment Funnel
Instead of asking a user to passively watch a video, this framework hooks them with a low-friction, multi-step interactive questionnaire that promises a customized diagnosis or specific roadmap at the end. [3] 

* 
* Why it works in 2026: Modern funnel infrastructure focuses heavily on real-time personalization, micro-segmentation, and zero-party data collection. A multi-step quiz filters and tags your traffic based on their exact answers, sending higher-quality data back to your ad platform's server-side APIs (like Meta or TikTok). [3, 4] 
* The Architecture: Keep the initial question extremely low-barrier (e.g., a simple multiple-choice click) to minimize friction. Use conditional routing logic behind the scenes so the questions dynamically change based on previous inputs. [1, 3] 
* The Call-to-Action (CTA): Capture the email address at the very end to "unlock" their custom profile or analysis report. Once captured, you can instantly drop them onto a personalized results page that pitches your primary solution. [1] 
* 

## 2. The Value-First Lead Magnet Funnel
This is a hyper-focused, low-friction squeeze page designed to exchange a high-utility, immediate-use digital asset (such as an analytical report, a plug-and-play template, or an interactive tool) for a user’s contact information. [1] 

* 
* Why it works in 2026: Attention spans are highly fragmented, and a segment of your traffic will always prefer skimming a text resource over watching a VSL. Delivering instant, un-gatekept value right at the point of signup builds rapid authority and conditions your new subscribers to open your follow-up sequence. [1] 
* The Architecture: Use a highly minimalist, mobile-first design. A strong, benefit-driven headline, 2-3 brief sub-bullets highlighting the key outcomes of the asset, and a single visible input field above the fold will yield the highest conversion rates. [5] 
* The Call-to-Action (CTA): Keep friction near zero by only asking for an email address to send the asset. [1]

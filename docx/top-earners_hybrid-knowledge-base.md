## Common Thread Among Top Earners

1. **Information + Implementation:** They don't just sell *content* (which gets pirated or reproduced by AI); they sell **speed**, **custom tooling/scripts**, or **live access**.
2. **Community as the Moat:** The discussion boards, peer accountability, and live streams keep members paying month after month even when they aren't actively consuming the archive.
3. **Decoupled Pricing:** High-earning technical and business communities charge $99/mo to $500/mo, requiring far fewer total members to hit 7-figure ARR compared to entertainment podcasts charging $5/mo.
